About Non-Ferrous Casting

Non-Ferrous Casting (NFC) is a proudly Zimbabwean industrial manufacturing company based in Norton, specializing in premium brass plumbing fittings, industrial valves, taps, mixers, and engineering castings.

For years, NFC has supplied reliable brass products to commercial, municipal, institutional, and infrastructure projects throughout Zimbabwe and beyond.

Through a combination of skilled craftsmanship, advanced manufacturing techniques, and strict quality standards, we continue to deliver durable engineering solutions designed for demanding industrial environments.

Precision Machining & Engineering

After casting, our components move through a rigorous machining process where skilled operators utilize heavy-duty industrial equipment, precision lathes, drilling systems, and specialized tooling.

Every fitting, valve, mixer, and plumbing component is machined to exact engineering specifications, ensuring dimensional accuracy, smooth operation, and long-term reliability in the field.

Our machining department combines technical expertise with modern manufacturing practices to transform raw castings into precision-engineered products ready for installation in commercial and industrial applications.

Quality Control & Finishing

Quality assurance is embedded throughout every stage of our manufacturing process. Each product undergoes detailed physical inspection to verify dimensional accuracy, finish quality, and functional performance.

Our finishing specialists carefully grind, polish, and refine components to meet NFC's exact standards while maintaining the strength and integrity of the material.

The result is a range of industrial brass products engineered for durability, corrosion resistance, and dependable performance even in harsh operating environments.
